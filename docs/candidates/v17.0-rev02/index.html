01 · What this is

A shared river-system perspective, not another parallel institution.

Converging Waters is focused on the Willkamayu–Vilcanota–Urubamba river system along its full course. It connects people, evidence and existing structures so different efforts can become mutually legible and more effective.

Territory

One connected river system

Start from the river as a whole, while keeping local territories, responsibilities and evidence distinct.

Method

Evidence + local knowledge + science

Bring environmental evidence, lived knowledge, monitoring, law, economics, education and public accountability into one usable picture.

Collaboration

Autonomous allies, connected by purpose

The project is currently led by Felipe Acha Fort, with World Genome Academy as its first scientific-technical convergence partner, and is expanding a network of autonomous allies.

↔

The operating thesis: do not duplicate institutions. Connect evidence, people and existing structures so better understanding can lead to better action — and action can be measured.

04 · Where you fit
