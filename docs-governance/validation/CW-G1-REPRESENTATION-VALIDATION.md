- `CONFIRM` — accurate and acceptable as written.
- `CORRECT` — retain the item with specified changes.
- `DEFER` — insufficient information or timing; revisit later.
- `REJECT` — do not use this representation.
- `NOT AUTHORIZED FOR PUBLIC USE` — may be useful internally but must not appear externally.
