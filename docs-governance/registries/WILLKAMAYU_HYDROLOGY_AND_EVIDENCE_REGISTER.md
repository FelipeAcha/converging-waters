## Canonical working convention

### VERIFIED

- `Willkamayu` is historically documented as the Inka name for the Urubamba river and is translated by Jean-Pierre Protzen as “sacred river.” Protzen also uses `Vilcanota` for the upper course and `Urubamba` downstream. This supports the cultural/historical identity of the river while showing that geographic naming conventions vary by source.
- Current Peruvian institutions also call the Vilcanota `Willkamayu` / `Wilkamayu` and refer to it as the sacred river of the Incas.
- Project working label for the connected main stem: **Willkamayu / Vilcanota–Urubamba**.
- Do not claim that `Vilcanota` is linguistically derived from or a corruption of `Willkamayu` without a dedicated primary linguistic source.
- Preserve spelling variants (`Willkamayu`, `Wilkamayu`, `Wilcamayu`) in source metadata. Website working spelling remains **Willkamayu** unless the team later adopts another convention.

## Hydrological facts relevant to the workstream

### VERIFIED

- The Huatanay is a principal tributary of the Vilcanota.
- Historical INRENA/ATDR inventory material locates the Huatanay mouth in the Vilcanota at the Huambutío sector.
- DIGESA’s 2007 Vilcanota–Urubamba monitoring network placed station `RV-06` 100 m downstream of the Huatanay discharge, followed by stations downstream of Pisac, Calca, Urubamba and Ollantaytambo. This is strong precedent for an attribution-oriented monitoring design around the confluence.
- The Vilcanota–Urubamba receives the Huatanay and later joins the Tambo to form the Ucayali.
- The Ucayali and Marañón form the Amazon; the Amazon continues to the Atlantic.
- Corrective rule: **do not state that the Vilcanota/Urubamba is a tributary of the Apurímac.** The Apurímac continues through the Ene/Tambo branch; the Tambo then joins the Urubamba to form the Ucayali.

### Hydrology sources

1. DIGESA, *Río Vilcanota–Urubamba 2007*: stations RV-01–RV-15; RV-06 is 100 m downstream of the Huatanay discharge; the document states that Vilcanota–Urubamba + Tambo form the Ucayali. Source: https://www.digesa.minsa.gob.pe/DEPA/rios/2007/vilcanota_07.pdf
2. ANA, 2023: Huatanay described as the principal tributary of the Vilcanota. Source: https://www.gob.pe/institucion/ana/noticias/772557-cusco-se-recogen-mas-de-45-toneladas-de-residuos-solidos-del-cauce-y-ribera-del-rio-huatanay
3. INRENA / ATDR-Cusco historical *Inventario de Fuentes de Agua Superficiales*: Huatanay mouth recorded at Huambutío. Primary repository copy still to be located; current indexed reproduction is a locator lead, not the final citation.
4. INEI, *Conociendo Ucayali*: Apurímac → Ene → Tambo; Tambo receives Urubamba and becomes Ucayali; Ucayali receives Marañón and becomes Amazon. Source: https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/web/biblioineipub/bancopub/Est/Lib0256/resum.htm

### Candidate monitoring logic for team review

- `M1 — Main-stem upstream reference`: Willkamayu/Vilcanota before receiving the Huatanay.
- `M2 — Tributary signal`: Huatanay immediately before confluence.
- `M3 — Mixed downstream signal`: Willkamayu/Vilcanota immediately below Huambutío.
- `M4+ — Corridor stations`: representative sites from Pisac through Ollantaytambo.

This is a hypothesis for scoping, not a finalized MRV network. Station selection must later account for access, seasonality, tributary inputs, wastewater discharges, hydromorphology, sampling protocol, laboratory requirements and community priorities.

## Water-quality evidence base — first tranche

### TIER A — institutional monitoring / plans

- **DIGESA 2007 — Río Vilcanota–Urubamba.** Established 15 stations. At RV-06: 100 m downstream of Huatanay discharge. Many monitored stations exceeded then-applicable limits for total and thermotolerant coliforms in Jan/Mar 2007. Source: https://www.digesa.minsa.gob.pe/DEPA/rios/2007/vilcanota_07.pdf
- **DIGESA monitoring archive.** Contains Río Huatanay 2006 and Río Vilcanota 2005/2007 monitoring entries. Source: https://www.digesa.minsa.gob.pe/DEPA/vigilancia_recursos_hidricos.asp
- **ANA 2025 — Urubamba–Vilcanota water-quality monitoring.** ANA reported a network of 160 control points across Vilcanota, Urubamba, Yavero and tributaries from La Raya toward Ucayali; field parameters included temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen and conductivity, with laboratory physicochemical and microbiological analysis. Source: https://www.gob.pe/institucion/ana/noticias/1178192-ana-culmina-monitoreo-de-calidad-de-agua-en-la-unidad-hidrografica-urubamba-vilcanota
- **ANA Plan de Gestión de Recursos Hídricos de la Cuenca Vilcanota Urubamba.** Approved by Resolución Jefatural N.° 0409-2022-ANA. Source: https://www.gob.pe/institucion/ana/normas-legales/3805057-0409-2022-ana

### TIER B — academic / open repository studies

- **Calvo Mamani & Polo Dolmos, UNSAAC, 2017 — Evaluación de la contaminación del río Huatanay.** Sampling Dec 2014–Nov 2015 using physical, chemical and bacteriological indicators; repository abstract reports values above ECAs and ICA from “mala” to “muy mala,” and explicitly notes influence on Vilcanota water quality. Source: https://repositorio.unsaac.edu.pe/handle/20.500.12918/2683
- **Arenas Pedraza, UNSAAC, 2019 — Calidad ambiental de Huancaro, T’ankarpata y Huillcarpay, tributaries of Huatanay.** Useful for upstream tributary/source characterization. Source: https://repositorio.unsaac.edu.pe/handle/20.500.12918/4753
- **UNSAAC, agricultural/heavy-metal study, 2019.** Evaluated Hg, As, Cr, Ni, Pb and Zn in water/soil under irrigation from the Huatanay sub-basin; relevant because it does not simply replicate the microbiological narrative and may constrain which contaminants are actually material. Source: https://repositorio.unsaac.edu.pe/handle/20.500.12918/4259
