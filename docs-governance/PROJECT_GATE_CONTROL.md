## 1. Operating principles

1. **Incremental change only.** Preserve approved material and modify only the components required by the active gate.
2. **One active gate at a time.** Parallel discovery is allowed, but no downstream gate is treated as complete until its entry criteria are met.
3. **No silent assumptions.** Roles, commitments, budgets, legal positions, equipment, travel, pilot sites, and institutional relationships remain provisional until explicitly validated.
4. **Purpose before technology.** Territorial need and the decision to be informed must precede equipment selection or data collection.
5. **Source traceability.** Every material claim must be tied to a source, explicit participant confirmation, or clearly labeled inference.
6. **Autonomy by default.** WGA, Amazonas Sagrada, and Willkamayu remain autonomous initiatives. Converging Waters is a collaboration layer, not an umbrella organization or merger.
7. **Team-safe communication.** Private conversations, internal production notes, and technical workflow lessons are not included in team-facing materials unless intentionally approved.
8. **Visual QA is mandatory.** Images use contain/fit rather than crop; tables have internal padding; slide text uses bullets or numbering with sufficient spacing; no completion claim is made before PPTX and PDF review.
9. **Reversible delivery.** Each material version is separately named and preserved. Prior approved versions remain available for rollback.
10. **Learning closure.** Any omission, visual failure, broken link, unsupported claim, or repeated correction must produce a reusable prevention rule.

## 6. Decision and evidence labels

Every material item must carry one label:
- **CONFIRMED** — explicitly validated by the responsible person or authoritative source.
- **PROPOSED** — recommended for discussion.
- **EXPLORATORY** — possible, but insufficiently validated.
- **DEFERRED** — intentionally postponed to a later gate.
- **BLOCKED** — cannot proceed until a named dependency is resolved.
- **REJECTED** — reviewed and intentionally not pursued.

## 7. Change protocol

For every material change:
1. identify the active gate;
2. state the exact baseline;
3. list only the requested delta;
4. classify direct cause, dependent change, unrelated validated state, and unknowns;
5. implement the smallest reversible change;
6. read back the result;
7. validate against gate exit criteria;
8. record evidence and remaining commitments;
9. advance only when the gate is closed or explicitly deferred.
